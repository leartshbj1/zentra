# Zentra Gestion

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

React interface packaged with Tauri on Windows, macOS, iOS and Android. Shared web UI with touch adaptations; an iOS viewport preview is not native installation evidence.

## Users
Small Swiss businesses and their collaborators, managing clients, projects, sales documents, purchases, accounting and payroll in a shared company space.

## Product Purpose
Make daily management understandable and quick. Automation connects received information from Support with actions in Gestion, while exposing actual results and exceptions requiring a person.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Company-scoped permissions, subscriptions, synchronization, audit records and explicit approval requirements must remain intact during design work. No invented activity, savings, statistics or autonomous actions. Use Projet and Projets. Preserve French, German, Italian and English, dark/light appearance and iPhone safe areas.

## Brand Commitments
The user requests a restrained Apple-like application, readable and light on mobile and desktop. The existing Zentra identity and green accent remain. On 2026-09-23 the user explicitly chose complete Automation activity first, with settings separated, and requested a working interactive preview directly.

## Product Principles
- Activity is factual and belongs to the selected company.
- Everyday tasks take priority over configuration text.
- Progressive detail keeps important controls visible.
- Collaboration and existing business rules remain unchanged by presentation work.

## Evidence on Hand
AutomationHub, AutomationBrief, AutomationControlCentre and AutomationSettings expose the current interactions. Development fixtures contain synthetic data only. Native release 1.83.0 is the starting point.
